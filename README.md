# Discovery media inventory

Use this free worksheet to keep track of recordings in a discovery production. It gives a reviewer one place to see which files opened, where audio began, and which passages need a second listen. The CSV opens in Excel, Numbers, or LibreOffice.

**[Open the free discovery media inventory](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/static/tools/discovery-media-inventory/index.html)** to start with the recordings on your device. The tool fills in filenames and reads durations where your browser supports the format. Add your review notes and export a CSV before closing the tab. Your files and notes stay on your device.

Prefer to start in a spreadsheet? [Download the blank CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joshuamehaffey703-ai/discovery-media-inventory/main/media-inventory.csv) and save a working copy in your matter folder.

To budget for a transcript, paste the durations for one case into the [bodycam transcription cost calculator](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/static/tools/transcription-cost-calculator/index.html?utm_source=github-worksheet&utm_medium=referral). It totals the recordings and gives you a printable base-price estimate.

## Fill in one row per recording

1. Assign a short ID, such as `REC-001`. Copy the original filename and its folder or disc label. Two cameras can produce files with the same name.
2. Try a working copy in your usual player. Record the player, whether the file opened, and the duration it displayed. Enter `Unknown` for a duration you could not measure.
3. Use elapsed time from the start of that file for the first audible sound and the review range. Enter times as `hh:mm:ss`. If you also need the clock time visible on the footage, use the [video timestamp calculator](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/static/tools/video-timestamp-calculator/index.html) and label that time separately in your notes.
4. Describe an observation someone else can check. For example, “Picture begins at 00:00:00. First audible speech at 00:00:31.” Use the follow-up column for a specific action, such as “Compare with second camera.”

| If you encounter this | What to record | Worked example or reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bodycam picture starts before sound | First audible point and whether sound continues after it | [Why bodycam footage can begin with silence](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/resources/bodycam-no-audio-first-30-seconds) |
| A surveillance export has a `.dav` extension | Exact filename, player tried, and the error it showed | [How to open a DAV surveillance export](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/resources/play-dav-video-file) |
| A call needs a transcript excerpt | Recording ID and elapsed start and end of the passage | [See a timestamped 911 call transcript](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/sample/call-911) |

If the production also includes a phone report, the [UFDR review checklist (PDF)](https://mehaffeytranscription.com/static/guides/ufdr-review-checklist.pdf) has room to record the report version, time zone, filters, and an attachment you expected to find.
